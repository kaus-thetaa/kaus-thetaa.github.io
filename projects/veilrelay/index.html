VeilRelay

A privacy layer for AI prompts.

Some thingsstay private.

Concept studyVeilRelay

The problem.

Sensitive text can leave your application inside an otherwise ordinary AI prompt.

What I built.

A self-hosted OpenAI-compatible proxy that replaces detected sensitive values with reversible typed placeholders.

Pattern detectors and validators inspect supported text before forwarding it. Known placeholders are restored in normal and streamed responses. The project includes a threat model and reproducible benchmark artifacts.

What it does.

Request-scoped mapping, streamed response support and value-free audit logs.

The constraints.

Alpha software. Detection is incomplete and does not guarantee privacy or compliance. Current benchmark results are a small synthetic smoke test, not production accuracy.
